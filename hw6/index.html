About MercyMed

MercyMed is a medical center in Bishkek that brings evidence-based, international medicine to Kyrgyzstan. We care for the whole person, body and spirit, and we invest in training the next generation of local medical professionals.

Our mission

Our vision

Medical services

Start with a consultation. Our doctors will guide you to the tests and follow-up you need.

Doctor consultations

Appointments with our doctors for new complaints, ongoing conditions and general check-ups.

Laboratory tests

Blood and other lab tests. Results are delivered by our lab partner, Express Plus, and can be accessed with a QR code.

Ultrasound & diagnostics

Diagnostic examinations, including ultrasound, to support an accurate diagnosis.
